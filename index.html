How your data is protected

  • Without an account, everything stays in this browser.
  • With an account, each saved scenario is locked in your browser with your vault passphrase (AES-256) before it is sent. The server only stores scrambled text it cannot read.
  • The database also only lets you reach your own rows.
  • Your vault passphrase is never sent anywhere. If you forget it, your saved scenarios cannot be recovered.
  • On a shared computer, use "Clear this device" when you finish.
